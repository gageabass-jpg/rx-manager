SMS / Text Messaging Terms

Last updated: September 2026

Program description

Rx Manager sends automated medication refill-reminder text messages to the mobile number you provide, notifying you when one of your medications is due to reorder and including a link to refill.

Consent

By providing your mobile number and checking the consent box on our sign-up page, you agree to receive recurring automated reminder texts at that number. Consent is not a condition of any purchase.

Message frequency & cost

Message frequency varies and depends on your medication schedule — typically a few messages per month, only when a medication is due. Message and data rates may apply per your mobile carrier plan.

Opt-out and help

Reply STOP at any time to cancel; you will receive one confirmation message and no further texts. Reply HELP for assistance, or email gage.a.bass@gmail.com.

Carriers

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

See our Privacy Policy. We do not sell or share your phone number.
