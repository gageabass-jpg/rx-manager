RX MANAGER

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Rx Manager is a personal medication-tracking tool operated by an individual. This policy explains how information is handled for its text-message (SMS) refill-reminder feature.

What we collect

  • The mobile phone number you provide to enable reminders.
  • The date/time you gave consent, and your browser's user-agent string, kept as a record of that consent.
  • Your medication list, which you enter yourself, used only to determine when to remind you.

How we use it

Your phone number is used solely to send you the medication refill-reminder texts you requested. Your medication data is used only to calculate reorder timing for those reminders. We do not use this information for marketing.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your phone number or reminder opt-in with anyone. Text messages are delivered through our messaging provider (Twilio) solely to transmit the messages you asked to receive. No mobile information is shared with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes.

Storage

Data is stored in a private database (Supabase) controlled by the operator, restricted to server-side access.

Opting out

Reply STOP to any reminder text to unsubscribe at any time. Reply HELP for help. You can also request removal by emailing the contact below.
